Key Quarterly Trends to Note
Q1 FY26 was a soft quarter — Revenue down 7.3% YoY, PAT down 42%, EBITDA margin compressed to 18.7%. This was due to muted hose volume, lower SS hose revenue (−25.6% YoY) and elevated D&A from new capex. Q2 and Q3 showed strong recovery — EBITDA margins expanded to 23.5% and 23.6% respectively, with Q3 revenue surging 21.1% YoY. New products (Bellows, Pipe Skid, Data Centre) started contributing from Q3 FY26.

Net Working Capital Days — Trend
Observations
Q1 FY26 Spike
WC days spiked to 161.8 in Q1 due to slower revenue + higher inventory buildup from new product launches. This normalised significantly by Q2 (124.5 days) and Q3 (112.9 days) — the best level in FY26.
Q3 FY26 Improvement
All three drivers improved YoY in Q3: receivables down 14.8 days, payables reduced 6.3 days. This is the clearest indicator of operational efficiency recovery after the weak Q1.
